Questions, answered.

What does the company brain learn from?

The AI interactions your team has through Token Station: the prompts, the answers that worked, and the dead ends. Dead ends matter as much as successes, because they are the time the next person can skip.

Is it on by default?

No. The company brain is optional, and your team turns it on when it is ready.

Who owns the knowledge?

Your company. Each interaction becomes structured data owned by your company, not by Token Station or a model vendor.

Do we need to use Token Station?

Yes. The company brain is built from the AI interactions that pass through Token Station, your team's single gateway to 30+ models.
